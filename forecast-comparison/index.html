Reading these maps

Expected rainfall is shown in inches. Exceedance probabilities are shown as percentages for the same fixed rainfall threshold in both panels. Both fields are displayed at matching 0.25° grid locations using their common finite land cells; no additional interpolation is performed for this page.

This page compares the two postprocessed ANN-CSGD systems for 12- and 24-hour accumulations: expected rainfall and the probability of exceeding 0.5, 1 and 2 inches, plus 5 inches for 24-hour periods where published. The HRRR dashboard also provides 6-hour guidance. A 6-hour GEFS counterpart is not available in the reviewed daily workflow, so that selection explains the limitation and links to HRRR.

The 24-hour methods differ. GEFS probabilities come from stored Monte Carlo samples of the sum of two non-overlapping 12-hour CSGD distributions, assuming independence between periods. GEFS expected rainfall is the sum of their expectations. HRRR uses its directly predicted 24-hour CSGD distribution. The GEFS construction does not represent temporal dependence between the two periods. Sampling variability also affects the probabilities. The sample count is displayed with the selected 24-hour forecast.

Maps are generated from saved numerical forecast fields. Initialization times and lead intervals appear above both panels. Up to twelve recent HRRR runs are retained. A missing same-cycle source is labeled as waiting; no older GEFS run is substituted. These maps compare forecast guidance, not verified model skill.
