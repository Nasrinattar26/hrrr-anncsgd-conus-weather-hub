GEFS ANN-CSGD model

Three independently trained artificial neural networks generate censored shifted gamma precipitation distributions for 6-, 12- and 24-hour accumulations. Every displayed duration is predicted directly by its corresponding model.

Inputs and forecast windows

The input is NOAA GEFS ensemble-mean surface precipitation distributed on the 0.5° grid. Consecutive 6-hour increments supply duration-specific totals, which are mapped to the common 0.25° CONUS grid. Twelve precipitation, neighborhood, temporal and geographical predictors are standardized using statistics estimated from each model’s training records.

Predictive distribution

The feedforward architecture has 12 inputs, hidden layers of 128, 128 and 64 ELU neurons, and 3 linear outputs. Layer normalization and 5% dropout follow the first two hidden layers. The outputs are transformed into admissible censored shifted gamma parameters. Zero rainfall receives a probability mass; positive amounts follow the shifted gamma component.

Expected rainfall and exceedance probabilities are calculated analytically from each duration’s distribution. The new 24-hour product does not sum independent 12-hour samples. Archived comparison runs may retain that earlier method; their run-specific method notes identify it.

Training and validation

The new models use disjoint historical training, validation and test periods. Windows extending across a calendar split boundary are removed. Source-specific scalers use training records only. The training objective is the complete closed-form CSGD CRPS, including its censoring-boundary term. Checkpoints are selected using validation loss.

Published model provenance and actual data splits records each trained checkpoint, data sources, record counts and diagnostic scores. Randomly sampled test diagnostics are labeled as sampled; they are not full-grid verification statistics. Model forecasts alone do not establish comparative forecast skill.

Thresholds, maps and downloads

Rainfall maps show inches; exceedance probabilities show percent. Duration-specific 2- and 5-year rainfall thresholds reuse the audited WPC threshold grids used by the HRRR system. Missing thresholds remain missing.

GRIB2 packages contain one file per accumulation window, with explicit initialization and start/end forecast hours. Expected amounts and fixed-threshold probabilities use standard interval templates. Probabilities for spatially varying ARI thresholds use documented local fields; each file’s JSON sidecar describes their variables and units.

GEFS–HRRR comparisons

New comparisons match initialization, accumulation window, fixed threshold and common finite grid cells. The comparison horizon is 48 hours, which both systems support; GEFS’s standalone dashboard continues to 192 hours. New run metadata identifies direct duration-specific distributions. Archived runs retain their original methods and availability notes.
