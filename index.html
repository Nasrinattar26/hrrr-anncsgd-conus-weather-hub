Duration-specific ANN-CSGD postprocessing

Each model receives twelve predictors describing HRRR precipitation, neighborhood structure, forecast lead, seasonality, valid hour, latitude, and longitude.

The neural network predicts the parameters of a censored-shifted gamma distribution, producing expected precipitation and threshold-exceedance probabilities. Models were evaluated independently on held-out 2025 MRMS observations.
