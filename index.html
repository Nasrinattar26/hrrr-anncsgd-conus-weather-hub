Method

Duration-specific ANN-CSGD postprocessing

Each model receives twelve predictors describing HRRR precipitation, neighborhood structure, forecast lead, seasonality, valid hour, latitude, and longitude. The neural network predicts the parameters of a censored-shifted gamma distribution, providing expected precipitation and full threshold-exceedance probabilities.

Models were selected using 2024 validation data and evaluated independently on held-out 2025 observations from MRMS.
