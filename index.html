About this forecast

HRRR inputs, ANN-CSGD probabilities

One HRRR initialization supplies precipitation predictors on a 0.25° grid. A neural network predicts a rainfall distribution at each land grid point. Expected rainfall and exceedance probabilities are calculated from that distribution.

Read the forecast and verification methods

Separate models handle 6-, 12- and 24-hour accumulations. The operational forecast does not combine time-lagged HRRR members.
