How these comparisons are matched

Both models use the same initialization, accumulation interval, verifying MRMS observations, common valid land cells and rainfall threshold. All paired leads end within the common forecast horizon through 48 hours. The 12- and 24-hour windows advance every six hours and overlap.

Research results use frozen study checkpoints. Recent results describe the operational checkpoints recorded for each issued forecast. Their scores are presented separately. Overlapping windows and spatial dependence mean that point counts are not independent sample sizes.

Scope of the displayed results

Only calculated and validated diagnostics appear in these tables. Additional interval, PIT, spatial-neighborhood and uncertainty analyses are separate extensions.

The historical four-window operational pilot and the HRRR predictor-resolution experiment remain available in the earlier comparison view; their samples and references differ from the annual study.

Precipitation errors are in millimeters. Operational rainfall maps use inches. One inch equals 25.4 mm.
